Blog

Welcome to the blog! This is a space for reflection, exploration, and conversation around Japanese literature. Here, you’ll find thoughtful reviews, personal essays, and honest critiques that dive beneath the surface of the stories we read. Whether you're discovering a new author or revisiting a classic, this is where we unpack those lingering feelings after the final page.

Reviews

In the Reviews section, I share honest impressions of the books I’ve read: what moved me, what challenged me, and what stayed with me long after the last page. These aren’t just summaries, but reflections on how each story resonates, surprises, or even unsettles. Whether glowing or critical, every review is rooted in a genuine love for Japanese literature.

Critiques

In this section, I take a closer look at the details of what worked, what didn’t, and why. It’s less about tearing things apart and more about asking questions, exploring themes, and thinking through the choices an author (or translator) made.

Essays

The Essays section is where I slow down and reflect. Sometimes it’s about a theme that keeps showing up in the books I read, other times it’s a personal connection I felt to a story or character.
