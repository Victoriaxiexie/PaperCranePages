Learning

Japanese literature has a long and rich history that stretches back over a thousand years. Writing first made its way to Japan from China in the 5th century, by way of the Korean peninsula. Some of the earliest surviving texts, like the Kojiki and Nihon Shoki, were compiled in the 8th century and give us a glimpse into the country’s mythologies, politics, and cultural roots (japan-guide). This section is a space to dive into both the classical foundations and the evolution into modern storytelling, whether you’re just getting started or looking to deepen your appreciation of Japanese literature through its historical context.

Classical Literature

This page explores the world of classical Japanese literature—from poetry and court tales to early prose and dramatic forms. A space to discover the stories, styles, and voices that shaped Japan’s literary beginnings.

Step into the world of modern Japanese literature, where tradition meets the surreal. This page dives into stories shaped by magical realism, science fiction, and the quiet strangeness of everyday life.
