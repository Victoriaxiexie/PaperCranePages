About

Welcome to a little corner of the internet where Japanese literature comes to life—quietly, curiously, and just a bit magically.

This site is for anyone who's ever wanted to slip into stories where cats talk, time bends, cafes exist between worlds, and the ordinary feels just a little enchanted. Japanese literature has a special way of blending whimsy with depth, and whether you're new to it or already in love, this is a place to start exploring.

Think of it as a bookshelf you can wander through when you’re not quite sure what you’re looking for, but you know you want to feel something.
