Themes

When indulging Japanese Literature, readers may start to enter a bubble of the world outlined and written in the pages between the front and back cover. Emotions and feelings arise based on feeling a connection with the character's personality, journey or relationships. Key themes include:

Cats and Cafes

Open any Toshikazu Kawaguchi and you have entered a cozy, quiet and coffee aromatic cafe. If you get lucky, a cat will appear to be your companion. Novels and novellas of this theme have become popularized amongst many authors offering an inviting and feel-good story.

Nostalgia

Characters experience nostalgia and readers feel nostalgic after closing these books.

Melancholy

Captures deep explorations of character, emotion, and society, often addressing the complexities of human relationships and the inner workings of the mind. Writers like Sayaka Murata and Natsume Sōseki have shaped this genre with works that delve into universal human experiences, often set against the backdrop of Japan's evolving social norms.

Thought-provoking

Timeless insights into human nature and aristocratic life capture the beauty, intrigue, and complexities of the Heian period. It lays the foundation for Japan's literary tradition, influencing everything from poetry to modern narrative forms.
